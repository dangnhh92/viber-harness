# Writing and copy conventions

Applies to all published prose: blog posts, App Store / marketing copy, app descriptions. NOT chat formatting (bold and bullets there aid scanning, keep them). Goal: reads like a human wrote it, kill AI tells.

- **No em-dash or en-dash**: readers take them as a machine tell. Use a comma, colon or parentheses, or rewrite the sentence. Grep for both before publishing.
- **No sycophancy / chatbot filler**: "great question", "let me know", "hope this helps", "certainly".
- **Banned AI words**: delve, tapestry, testament, showcase, crucial, pivotal, landscape, enhance, boasts, seamless, realm, robust, leverage, elevate, foster. Use plain words (explore, improve, area, key, has, proof).
- **No copula dressing**: "serves as / boasts / features / stands as" → "is / has".
- **No negative parallelism** as a hook: "not just X, it's Y", "not X but Y" → state it straight.
- **No significance or -ing inflation**: "a testament to", "pivotal moment", "symbolizing / reflecting / showcasing" → say the actual thing.
- **No signposting / rhetorical openers**: "let's dive in", "the real question is", "at its core", "Honestly?", "Here's the thing" → open with the claim.
- **No manufactured drama**: don't stack short fragments for effect; no "X is the Y of Z" aphorism formulas; vary sentence length naturally.
- **Hedge once** (one "may", not "could potentially possibly"). **Cut filler** ("in order to" → "to", "due to the fact that" → "because").
- **No uplift endings** ("the future looks bright") → end on something concrete.
- **Mechanics**: straight quotes not curly, sentence-case headings, no mechanical bolding of terms.
- Copy from a real source (another app's privacy, a template) must be made TRUE for this app before publishing; a mismatch is its own slop.
